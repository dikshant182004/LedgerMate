5 Best Free Splitwise Alternatives in 2026 (No Ads, No Daily Limits)

Published September 2026 · 6 min read

If you've opened Splitwise recently, you've probably encountered the dreaded popup: "You have reached your daily limit of 3 expenses. Upgrade to Splitwise Pro for $39.99/year." For millions of roommates, couples, and travelers who relied on the app for over a decade, this paywall — combined with 10-second timer countdown ads — has triggered a massive migration.

Fortunately, you don't have to pay $40 a year just to log who paid for tacos. Here is an honest review of the top 5 free Splitwise alternatives in 2026.

1. LedgerMate (Best Overall for Frictionless Splitting)

Free · No Account Required · No Ads

LedgerMate was built specifically to solve the modern frustration with expense splitting apps: it requires no app download and no account creation to start. You create a group on the web, share the link via WhatsApp or iMessage, and your friends join instantly simply by typing their name.

Pros:

  • 100% Free & Unlimited: Add as many expenses per day as you want. Zero ads.
  • Real-Time Live WebSockets: When someone adds a receipt at the table, everyone's screen updates instantly without pulling to refresh.
  • Debt Simplification: Minimizes total transactions automatically so the group settles up in the fewest payments.
  • Auto-Expiring Privacy: One-off weekend trips and dinner bills can be scheduled to clean themselves up after 1 day or 1 week so your data isn't held forever.

Cons:

  • Does not support in-app bank payment transfers (uses standard PayPal/Venmo/UPI external settle-ups).

2. Tricount (Best for Traditional Mobile App Users)

Free Tier · Mobile App · Owned by bunq

Tricount has long been popular in Europe. Acquired by European challenger bank bunq, it allows groups to log expenses without every participant needing an account. It generates a shareable link that opens in the app.

Pros:

  • Works offline when traveling in areas without cell reception.
  • Simple debt calculation and clean UI.

Cons:

  • Increasing prompts to open a bunq European banking account.
  • Web browser interface is limited compared to native mobile apps.

3. Splid (Best for Offline Wilderness Travel)

Free with One-Time Pro Upgrade

Splid is an indie German app that has earned high ratings for its simplicity and clean offline functionality. You don't need to register an account, and it handles multiple currencies nicely.

Pros:

  • Fast offline mode; syncs when back on Wi-Fi.
  • Good multi-currency support with offline exchange rates.

Cons:

  • Requires everyone to download the mobile app from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Advanced features like PDF/Excel exports require an in-app purchase.

4. Settle Up (Best for Cross-Platform Desktop & Mobile)

Freemium with Banner Ads

Settle Up has been around for many years and supports iOS, Android, and Web. It supports multiple split types (by percentage, shares, or exact amounts) and transaction history logs.

Pros:

  • Handles complex split options like percentage weights and itemized bills.
  • PayPal and local payment link integration.

Cons:

  • The free version has persistent banner ads and occasional video ads.
  • Requires all members to log in with an email or social account.

5. Google Sheets / Excel (Best for Ultimate Customization)

100% Free · Manual Math

For tech-savvy roommates, a shared Google Sheet remains a timeless fallback. There are hundreds of free templates available that track expenses and use pivot tables to calculate totals.

Pros:

  • Infinitely customizable; zero fear of an app shutting down or adding paywalls.
  • Great for auditing yearly household budgets.

Cons:

  • Terrible mobile experience (pinch-zooming spreadsheets on a 6-inch phone at dinner is painful).
  • No automatic debt simplification algorithm unless you write custom Apps Script formulas.

Verdict: If you want the speed of an app without making everyone download software or hit 3-expense limits, LedgerMate is the fastest, cleanest free solution in 2026.
