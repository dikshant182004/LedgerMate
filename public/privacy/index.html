LedgerMate Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026 · Built around radical data minimization, zero-signup anonymous groups, and secure BYOK execution.

The LedgerMate Privacy Promise in 30 Seconds: We never sell your personal data. We do not require account registration to split expenses. Quick groups self-destruct on automated timers. If you use our AI Calculation Agent with Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK), your Google AI Studio API key is never stored on our database or written to server logs.

1. Introduction & Data Controller

LedgerMate ("we", "our", or "us"), available at https://tryledgermate.in/, provides real-time group expense splitting, 55+ interactive mathematical and financial calculators, and a neuro-symbolic AI Calculation Agent. This Privacy Policy governs your use of our web services, mobile progressive web applications (PWAs), and APIs.

For visitors in the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA), LedgerMate operates as a Data Controller committed to General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) compliance under the principle of data minimization (Article 5(1)(c) GDPR).

2. Information We Collect (and What We Do NOT Collect)

A. Anonymous Expense Groups (No Sign-Up)

When you create or join an expense group without an account:

  • Names: Display names you enter for yourself or group members (e.g., "Alex", "Flat 4B"). You can use pseudonyms or initials.
  • Transaction Details: Item descriptions (e.g., "Groceries", "Tacos"), amounts, and split allocations.
  • Ephemeral Storage: These groups are assigned an expiry duration (10 minutes, 1 hour, or 1 day). Upon expiration, our background cron workers permanently purge all member names, expenses, and balances from our database.

B. Signed-In Users (Google OAuth)

Signing in is 100% optional. If you choose to sign in with Google to maintain permanent groups or view cross-device calculation history:

  • We receive your Google public profile name, verified email address, avatar picture URL, and Google unique subject ID (`sub`).
  • We generate a cryptographically random session token stored in an `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` cookie. We never receive or store your Google account password.

C. AI Calculation Agent & BYOK API Key Security

Our AI Calculation Agent allows users to perform plain-English financial calculations using Google Gemini models:

  • Zero Persistence for API Keys: When you provide your Google AI Studio API key, it is kept in your browser's local session memory. During API calls, it is dispatched strictly via an encrypted HTTP header (`x-api-key`).
  • No Server-Side Logging: Our backend runs an explicit sanitizer (`sanitizeApiKey`) that strips and redacts API keys before request processing. API keys are never written to Cloudflare D1 databases, Durable Objects, or server execution logs.
  • Query History: Calculation inputs and the verified algebraic outputs are saved to your private history table only if you are signed in, allowing you to review past calculations or request refinements. You can delete individual calculation records at any time.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to fulfill core service functions:

  1. Real-Time Synchronization: Broadcasting balance updates and expense entries across group members over secure WebSockets.
  2. Debt Simplification: Running greedy debt minimization algorithms to settle shared bills with the fewest possible bank transfers.
  3. Service Integrity & Abuse Prevention: Enforcing in-memory rate limits (maximum 30 calculations per minute) to prevent bot flooding.
  4. Product Improvement: User-submitted ratings and qualitative feedback submitted via our Feedback Hub are used to fix bugs and build requested features.

4. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

LedgerMate uses minimal, privacy-preserving browser storage:

  • Session Cookie (`session`): A secure authentication cookie active for 30 days only if you explicitly sign in.
  • Theme Preference (`theme`): Stored in HTML5 `localStorage` so your choice of light or dark mode persists across reloads without tracking your identity.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Cookies: LedgerMate serves zero third-party ads. We do not use Google AdSense, Meta Pixel, or third-party retargeting cookies.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Processors

We partner only with industry-leading infrastructure providers that maintain strict SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance:

  • Cloudflare Inc.: Global CDN edge network, Workers serverless compute, D1 distributed SQL, and Durable Objects.
  • Google Cloud / Google AI Studio: Gemini model inference and Google Search Grounding for live tax/exchange constants. Handled under Google Cloud's enterprise data protection agreements.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA/CPRA

Under international privacy regulations, you retain full ownership of your data:

  • Right to Access: You can view all active groups and calculation history directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can delete any calculation record, expense, or group at any time. Anonymous groups automatically delete themselves.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit expense amounts, names, or currency settings instantly.
  • Data Portability: Export your complete group ledger to CSV or printable summary in 1 click from the group topbar.

7. Data Retention Policy

Data Category Retention Period Purge Mechanism
Anonymous Quick Groups 10 minutes, 1 hour, or 1 day (user-selected) Automated scheduled worker cron job
Signed-In Groups 1 week to Permanent (until deleted by owner) User manual deletion or account deletion
Calculation Agent History Retained in user account until deleted Instant deletion via API/UI trash icon
BYOK API Keys 0 seconds (in-flight memory only) Never written to disk or logs

8. Security Measures

All data in transit is encrypted using Modern TLS 1.3 with HTTPS enforcement. Group invitations use cryptographically signed tokens (`signExpenseId`), and our security disclosure protocol is published at /.well-known/security.txt and /security/calc-agent/.

9. Contact Our Privacy Team

For privacy inquiries, GDPR data requests, or security disclosures, submit a ticket via our Feedback & Support Hub or review our About & Research Board Page.
