💡 Common Aspect Ratios in Modern Media

  • 16:9 (1.78:1): Standard for YouTube, HDTV, PC monitors, and television broadcasting.
  • 9:16 (0.56:1): Inverted widescreen format for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
  • 21:9 (2.39:1): Anamorphic widescreen for cinematic movies and ultrawide gaming monitors.
  • 4:5 (0.80:1): Maximizes feed real estate in Instagram vertical posts (1080 × 1350).

How to Calculate Aspect Ratios (Greatest Common Divisor)

To reduce any pixel resolution to its simplest aspect ratio, divide both the width and height by their greatest common divisor (GCD). For example, with 1920 and 1080, the GCD is 120, yielding 16:9.
