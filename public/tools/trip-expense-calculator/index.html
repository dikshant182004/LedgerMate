How to split trip expenses fairly

The easiest way to split a group trip is to log every expense as it happens, along with who actually paid for it, rather than trying to remember and divide everything at the end. Once every cost is logged, the fair split isn't "divide the total by the number of people" — it's each person's share of the total, compared against what they already paid. This calculator does that math for you, and then simplifies the result into the smallest number of payments needed so nobody has to send five different small transfers to five different people.

FAQ

How do I calculate a trip expense split?

Add each person on the trip, then log every expense with who paid for it. The calculator totals what everyone paid versus what they owe, then works out the smallest number of payments needed to settle up.
