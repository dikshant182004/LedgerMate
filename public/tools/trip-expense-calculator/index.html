How to Split Trip Expenses Fairly

The easiest way to split a group trip is to log every expense as it happens, along with who actually paid for it, rather than trying to remember and divide everything at the end. Once every cost is logged, the fair split isn't "divide the total by the number of people" — it's each person's share of the total, compared against what they already paid.

Debt Simplification: Instead of settling every individual purchase with 15 confusing bank transfers, debt simplification balances out mutual debts so 4 people can settle up in just 2 quick payments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a trip expense split?

Add each person on the trip, then log every expense with who paid for it. The calculator totals what everyone paid versus what they owe, then works out the smallest number of payments needed to settle up.
