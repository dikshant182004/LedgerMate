💡 The Rule of 72 for Fast Compounding Math

The Compound Interest Formula

Compound interest with regular additions is calculated as: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × (((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)), where P is principal, PMT is recurring payment, r is annual interest rate, n is compounding frequency, and t is time in years.
