💡 How to Measure Correctly (Avoid the #1 Error)

  • Neck: Measure just below the larynx (Adam's apple), with the tape sloping slightly downward to the front. Keep shoulders relaxed.
  • Waist (Men): Measure horizontally around the waist directly at navel height. Do NOT suck in your stomach.
  • Waist & Hips (Women): Measure the waist at the narrowest natural indentation (above the belly button) and hips at the widest point around the buttocks.

Understanding US Navy Body Fat Equations

The US Department of Defense developed these circumference equations to replace expensive hydrostatic testing:

  • Men: 495 ÷ (1.0324 - 0.19077 × log10(Waist - Neck) + 0.15456 × log10(Height)) - 450
  • Women: 495 ÷ (1.29579 - 0.35004 × log10(Waist + Hip - Neck) + 0.22100 × log10(Height)) - 450
