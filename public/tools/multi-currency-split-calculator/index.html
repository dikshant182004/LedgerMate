    How to Split Multi-Currency Travel Expenses

    International backpacking, study-abroad excursions, and global vacations almost always involve multiple currencies. One friend pays for train tickets in Euros, another fronts the rental van in British Pounds, and another buys dinners in Thai Baht.

    1. The "Base Currency" Rule

    Never try to settle cross-currency debts one by one. Instead, convert all expenses into a single base currency (like USD, EUR, or INR) that everyone is comfortable transacting in. This eliminates circular exchange calculations.

    2. Use Fair Mid-Market Rates

    Rather than using airport kiosk markups (which can be 10-15% inflated), use mid-market rates or the exact conversion shown on the cardholder’s bank notification.

    3. Why LedgerMate Replaces Splitwise Pro

    Splitwise locks multi-currency auto-conversion behind its $39.99/year subscription. LedgerMate believes friends traveling together should never have to pay a subscription fee just to divide hotel bills fairly.
