📖 Prorated Rent Concepts & Legal Rules
Educational Guide

Exact Days vs 30-Day Banker Rule vs 365-Day Method

When prorating rent, the formula divides your monthly rent by a divisor to arrive at your daily rate. Three distinct divisors are accepted in property law:

  • Exact Calendar Days (Most Fair): Uses the precise number of days in the month (28, 29, 30, or 31). A $2,400 rent in 31-day October produces a daily rate of $77.42. In a 30-day month like April, the daily rate is $80.00. In a 28-day February, the daily rate is $85.71.
  • 30-Day Banker's / Commercial Rule: Treats every month as having exactly 30 days (`Rent ÷ 30`). In Germany (BGB § 556b) and several US commercial leases, this standardizes accounting, but can slightly overcharge tenants during 31-day months.
  • Annual Average 365-Day Method: Institutional property management software (like Yardi, AppFolio, RealPage) frequently uses `(Monthly Rent × 12) ÷ 365 = Daily Rate`. For $2,400/mo, the daily rate is $78.90 every single day of the year.
Exact Method: Daily Rate = Monthly Rent ÷ Days in Month
Banker Method: Daily Rate = Monthly Rent ÷ 30
Annual Method: Daily Rate = (Monthly Rent × 12) ÷ 365
Example: Moving in on October 18 ($2,400 rent, 14 days left):
• Exact Days (31 days): $77.42 × 14 = $1,083.87
• 30-Day Banker Rule: $80.00 × 14 = $1,120.00 (Tenant pays $36.13 more)
• Annual 365 Rule: $78.90 × 14 = $1,104.66

The Move-In Day Rule: Do You Pay for the Day You Move In?

Under tenancy law across common law jurisdictions (US, UK, Canada, Australia), a tenant receives legal possession, rights of quiet enjoyment, and key access on the move-in date stated in the lease.

Because you hold legal keys and occupancy starting at 12:01 AM or the handover hour, the move-in date is always counted as a billable day. The mathematical formula for billable days is inclusive:

Billable Days = (Total Days in Month - Move-In Date Number) + 1

If you sign a lease beginning on July 10 in a 31-day month: `31 - 10 + 1 = 22` billable days. For move-outs, you pay through the key surrender date: moving out on the 10th equals 10 billable days.

Weekly Rent Proration in Australia and New Zealand

In Australia (NSW Fair Trading, Consumer Affairs Victoria, Residential Tenancies Authority Queensland) and New Zealand (Tenancy Services), rent is legally advertised and calculated on a weekly basis (e.g. $650 per week).

To convert weekly rent into daily rates or monthly equivalents:

Daily Rental Rate = Weekly Rent ÷ 7
Monthly Rent = (Weekly Rent ÷ 7) × 365 ÷ 12 = Weekly Rent × 4.3482
Australian Example: A Melbourne apartment rented at A$700 per week has a daily rate of exactly A$100.00 (`700 ÷ 7`). If moving in 4 days before the standard weekly direct debit cycle begins, the prorated charge is simply `4 × A$100.00 = A$400.00`.

Security Deposits, Advance Rent & Statutory Caps

A common dispute during mid-month move-ins is whether the first month's full rent or the prorated rent is paid on move-in day:

  • Standard Policy A (Prorated First Month): Tenant pays the prorated rent for the partial month plus the security deposit on move-in day. On the 1st of the following month, full regular rent begins.
  • Standard Policy B (Full Month First, Prorated Second Month): Many corporate landlords charge a full month's rent upfront on move-in day to ensure funds clear, and then deduct the prorated credit on the second calendar month's rent bill.
  • Jurisdictional Deposit Caps: In the UK, the Tenant Fees Act 2019 caps security deposits at 5 weeks' rent. In California (AB 12, effective 2024), security deposits are legally capped at 1 month's rent. In Ontario, landlords may only collect a rent deposit equal to one rental period (last month's rent).

Notice Periods & Mid-Month Move-Out Obligations

While moving in mid-month is straightforward, moving out mid-month requires careful review of your lease termination clause:

  • Fixed-Term Lease Expiry: If your 12-month lease ends on October 15, you only owe rent through October 15.
  • Month-to-Month Periodic Tenancies: In many jurisdictions (such as New York, California, and the UK), a standard 30-day notice requirement must coincide with a full rental period. Unless agreed in writing, giving notice on October 10 might not end your obligation until November 30.
  • Always obtain written agreement from your landlord confirming the exact move-out date and the prorated final rent amount before vacating.

Complete Guide to Prorated Rent: Formulas, International Standards & Legal Rights

Prorated rent is the proportionate adjustment of a tenant's rental fee when moving in after the first day of the billing cycle or moving out before the last day. Without proration, a tenant moving in on October 25 would be forced to pay an entire month's rent for merely seven days of actual physical occupancy.

Whether you are a tenant reviewing a new residential lease agreement, a landlord drafting an itemized move-in invoice, or roommates dividing shared housing costs, calculating prorated rent with mathematical precision prevents disputes and ensures fair housing compliance.

Step-by-Step Mathematical Calculation

To calculate prorated rent manually without software, follow this three-step workflow:

  1. Determine Billable Days: For a mid-month move-in, subtract the arrival date from the total days in that calendar month and add 1 (to include the move-in day itself): Billable Days = (Total Days - Move-In Date) + 1.
  2. Establish the Daily Rate: Divide the contracted monthly rent by the chosen divisor. Under the fair exact-days method, divide by 30 for April, June, September, November; divide by 28 (or 29 in leap years) for February; divide by 31 for all other months.
  3. Multiply Rate by Days: Multiply the daily rate by the billable days to determine the exact prorated rental balance due.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is prorated rent calculated mathematically?

Prorated rent is calculated by dividing monthly rent by the number of days in the specific month to find the daily rental rate, then multiplying that rate by the number of days the tenant occupies the property during that partial billing period.

Do you count the move-in day when prorating rent?

Yes. Legally, the tenant gains exclusive possession, rights to quiet enjoyment, and physical key access on the move-in date. Therefore, the move-in day is counted as a billable day under landlord-tenant law in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and most international jurisdictions.

How does prorating work in Australia and New Zealand?

In Australia and New Zealand, standard leases specify rent on a per-week basis. The statutory daily rate is calculated simply as Weekly Rent ÷ 7. For mid-week move-ins, multiply this daily rate by the exact days until the first regular rent cycle starts.

Can a landlord refuse to prorate rent?

A landlord can decline to start a lease mid-month and insist that the tenancy begin on the 1st. However, if the landlord grants possession and allows occupancy starting on a mid-month date, charging a full month's rent for a partial month is considered improper or illegal in almost all jurisdictions.

Which prorating method is the fairest between Exact Days and 30-Day Banker Rule?

The Exact Days in Month method is widely recognized as the fairest because it reflects the true calendar duration of the month. In a 31-day month like October or March, the 30-day method artificially inflates the daily rate, while in a 28-day February it undercharges.

Are utilities, storage lockers, and parking spaces also prorated?

Yes, flat-fee ancillary charges such as garage parking stalls, storage lockers, and bundled monthly utility fees should be prorated on the exact same daily rate proportion as the primary apartment rent.

What happens in a leap year like 2028?

During leap years, February has 29 days instead of 28. Our calculator dynamically determines the leap year calendar and divides by 29 days, ensuring you never overpay when moving in during late February.
