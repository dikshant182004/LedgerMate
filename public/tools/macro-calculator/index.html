💡 Evidence-Based Macro Rules

  • Why 1g per lb Isn't Always Necessary: A comprehensive meta-analysis (Morton et al., 2018) showed that protein intake beyond 1.62 g/kg (0.74g/lb) showed no additional benefits for muscle hypertrophy in resistance-trained athletes. During caloric deficits, 1.8 to 2.2 g/kg provides a safe buffer against catabolism.
  • Don't Drop Fats Below 20%: Dietary fat is required for testosterone, thyroid hormone synthesis, and fat-soluble vitamin absorption (A, D, E, K).

How to Calculate Your Macros Step-by-Step

Rather than using arbitrary percentage templates, sports dietitians calculate macros in this precise order:

  1. Determine Daily Calories: Set a deficit for fat loss or slight surplus for muscle gain.
  2. Anchor Protein: Set 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg of bodyweight (4 kcal/g).
  3. Anchor Fats: Allocate 20% to 30% of total calories to essential healthy fats (9 kcal/g).
  4. Fill Remaining Calories with Carbs: The leftover calories go entirely to carbohydrates to fuel gym performance and glycogen replenishment.
