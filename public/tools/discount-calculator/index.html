Mathematical Step-by-Step Breakdown

  1. Original starting price: $120.00
  2. First discount (30% off $120.00): -$36.00 → Intermediate price: $84.00
  3. Second discount (20% off remaining $84.00): -$16.80 → Pre-tax price: $67.20
  4. Notice: 20% was deducted from $84.00, NOT the original $120.00. That's why your total discount is 44%, not 50%!

💡 Shopping & Sale Stacking Mindmap

  • Order Doesn't Matter: Whether the store applies 30% first and then 20%, or 20% first and then 30%, multiplication is commutative: Price × 0.70 × 0.80 = Price × 0.56. The final price is identical either way!
  • Store Exclusions: Always check whether coupons apply to clearance items or if manufacturers enforce Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) exclusions.
