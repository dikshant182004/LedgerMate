Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard tipping percentage in restaurants?

In the United States and Canada, 15% to 20% is standard for dine-in table service. 15% represents acceptable service, 18% is typical, and 20% or higher is customary for great service.

Should tip be calculated before or after sales tax?

Etiquette experts recommend tipping on the pre-tax food and beverage subtotal. Sales tax is a government levy, so service staff should be compensated based on menu items ordered.

How do you split a tip between multiple people?

Add the total tip to the total bill and divide the grand sum by the number of people. Each person pays an equal portion of the food, tax, and gratuity.
