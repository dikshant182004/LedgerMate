💡 Real Estate Land Measurements in India & South Asia

  • 1 Gaj: 9 Sq Ft (1 Square Yard). 100 Gaj plot = 900 Sq Ft.
  • 1 Guntha: 1,089 Sq Ft (33 ft × 33 ft). Exactly 40 Gunthas equal 1 Acre. Widely used in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and AP/Telangana.
  • 1 Ground: 2,400 Sq Ft. Standard urban unit in Tamil Nadu and Chennai.
  • 1 Kanal: 5,445 Sq Ft (20 Marlas). 8 Kanals make 1 Acre. Standard in Punjab, Haryana, and Pakistan.
