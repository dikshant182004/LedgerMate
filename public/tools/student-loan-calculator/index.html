💡 Pro-Tip: Instruct Your Loan Servicer to Apply Extra Payments to Principal

  • Don't Let Them Advance the Due Date: By default, some loan servicers will count extra money as "prepaid future payments" rather than applying it immediately to reduce your loan principal balance. Always select "Apply directly to principal" in your student loan portal.
  • The Debt Avalanche Method: If you have multiple loans (e.g. Direct Unsubsidized at 7.05% vs Subsidized at 5.5%), pay the minimum on all and direct your entire extra payment toward the highest-interest loan first.

Why Even Small Extra Payments Make a Massive Difference

Because student loan interest accrues daily on the outstanding principal balance, every extra dollar paid directly to principal stops compounding interest immediately. Over several years, an extra $100 or $150 per month saves thousands in unnecessary finance charges.
