💡 The 20/4/10 Rule for Buying Cars

  • 20% Down Payment: Putting down 20% cushions against immediate depreciation the moment you drive off the lot.
  • 4-Year Maximum Loan: Limit loan terms to 48 months (4 years) to avoid excessive compounding interest and negative equity.
  • 10% of Gross Income: Total car expenses (payment + insurance + fuel) should not exceed 10% of your gross monthly income.

How Car Loan Financing is Calculated

Auto loan interest compounds on a simple interest daily basis. Monthly payments are derived from standard amortization: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n - 1).
