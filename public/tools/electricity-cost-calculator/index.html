💡 4 Practical Tips to Cut AC Bills by 25% to 35%

  • Set to 24°C instead of 18°C: Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) data proves that every 1°C increase in AC thermostat setting reduces electricity consumption by roughly 6%. Setting your AC at 24°C vs 18°C saves over 30% power.
  • Pair with a Ceiling Fan: Running a 75W ceiling fan circulates cool air effectively, allowing you to raise the AC temperature by 2°C with identical perceived comfort.
  • Clean Filters Every 2 Weeks: Clogged dust filters force the AC compressor to run 15% longer to pull the same airflow.

Real Cost to Run Your AC 8 Hours a Day: The Math

Electricity is billed per Kilowatt-Hour (kWh), where 1 kWh equals 1 unit on your meter. To find the cost: (Watts × Hours × Days ÷ 1,000) × Tariff Rate. For a 1,200 Watt 5-star AC running 8 hours at ₹8 per unit, that equals (1200 × 8 ÷ 1000) = 9.6 units/day or ₹76.80/day.
