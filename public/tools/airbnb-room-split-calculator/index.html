How to Split a Vacation Home Fairly

When booking an Airbnb or cabin rental with a group of friends, rarely are all bedrooms created equal. Forcing an even per-person split often leaves guests in bunk rooms feeling ripped off while the couple in the jacuzzi suite pays the same rate.

The 50/50 Rule: Private Space vs. Common Amenities

A vacation house consists of two distinct values:

  • 50% Private Bedroom Value: Allocated according to room quality (king bed, private bathroom, ocean view vs. twin bunk).
  • 50% Communal Space Value: Allocated equally per person (everyone enjoys the kitchen, living room, swimming pool, hot tub, and patio equally).

Frequently Asked Questions

How should couples pay for an Airbnb bedroom?

Couples share one bedroom, but count as two individuals using the kitchen, bathrooms, and living spaces. This calculator weights communal vs room spaces automatically.

What about cleaning and service fees?

Include all fees and taxes in the total stay cost so the full amount paid to Airbnb is divided proportionally.
