💡 How to Eliminate PMI Faster

  • Automatic PMI Cancellation: Under the Homeowners Protection Act, your lender must automatically cancel PMI once your loan balance reaches 78% of the original purchase value.
  • Early Cancellation at 80%: You can request PMI removal yourself once you pay your principal down to 80% LTV, or if market appreciation causes your home value to rise significantly (requires a new appraisal).

Understanding the 4 Components of PITI

When banks calculate your debt-to-income ratio, they look at your complete PITI payment, not just the base mortgage interest:

  • Principal: The portion that directly reduces your loan balance and builds equity.
  • Interest: The fee charged by your mortgage lender.
  • Taxes: County and municipal property taxes collected monthly into an escrow account.
  • Insurance: Hazard and flood insurance policies protecting the collateral.
