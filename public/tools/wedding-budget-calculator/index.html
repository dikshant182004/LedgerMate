💡 The Secret to Cutting Wedding Costs

  • Trimming the Guest List Trumps Trimming Vendors: Because catering, alcohol, table rentals, florals, and invitations are billed per person, cutting 20 guests frequently saves $3,000 to $6,000 instantly.
  • Protect Your 8% Contingency: Last-minute vendor gratuities, alterations, and overtime fees happen in 90%+ of weddings. Never allocate 100% of your budget upfront.

How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going into Debt

A successful wedding budget starts with a firm ceiling. Determine the non-negotiable elements (like photography or live music) first, and compromise on decorative accents or excessive party favors that guests rarely take home.
