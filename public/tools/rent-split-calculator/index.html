How to Split Rent Fairly Between Roommates

An even split works fine when every room is identical — but it stops feeling fair the moment one room is noticeably bigger, has its own bathroom, or gets better natural light.

Weighted Rent Formula: Each Roommate's Rent = (Their Room Weight ÷ Sum of All Room Weights) × Total Rent

A room weighted 1.3 against two rooms weighted 1.0 will pay an appropriately higher share, while the smaller rooms save money. Everyone agrees on the multipliers before signing the lease, preventing friction later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you split rent fairly when rooms are different sizes?

Give each room a weight based on size, private bathroom, or closet space, then split rent proportionally to those weights instead of splitting it evenly.

How do you split shared utilities like Wi-Fi and electricity?

Unless one roommate runs an energy-intensive server or space heater 24/7, utilities should almost always be split completely evenly among all roommates, regardless of room size.
