How to split rent fairly between roommates

An even split works fine when every room is roughly the same — but it stops feeling fair the moment one room is noticeably bigger, has its own bathroom, or gets better light. The common fix is a weighted split: assign each room a relative weight (its share of the total desirability, however your household defines that), and split the rent proportionally to those weights instead of dividing by headcount. A room weighted 1.3 against three rooms weighted 1.0 will pay a larger, but proportionate, share — everyone else's rent actually goes down slightly to compensate, rather than the smaller rooms subsidizing the big one.

FAQ

How do you split rent fairly when rooms are different sizes?

Give each room a weight based on size, private bathroom, or closet space, then split rent proportionally to those weights instead of splitting it evenly.
