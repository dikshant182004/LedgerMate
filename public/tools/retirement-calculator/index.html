💡 The Power of Starting 10 Years Earlier

  • The 8% Compounding Miracle: At 8% annual growth, money doubles roughly every 9 years. A 25-year-old who invests for just 10 years and stops often ends up with more wealth at 65 than a 35-year-old who invests continuously for 30 years.
  • Never Leave Match Money on the Table: If your employer offers a 50% match up to 6%, contributing less than 6% is literally declining part of your contractual compensation.

How the 4% Safe Withdrawal Rule Works

Originating from the landmark Trinity Study, the 4% rule demonstrates that withdrawing 4% of a diversified 60/40 or 75/25 stock/bond portfolio annually has survived every historical market crash, depression, and high-inflation period in modern history over 30-year spans.
