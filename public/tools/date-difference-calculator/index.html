Frequently Asked Questions

How does this calculator handle leap years?

The calculator uses standard Gregorian calendar logic which automatically accounts for February 29th in leap years (such as 2024 and 2028), ensuring 100% mathematical accuracy.

What is the difference between calendar days and business days?

Calendar days include all 7 days of each week (Monday through Sunday). Business days (or working days) only include Monday through Friday, completely excluding Saturdays and Sundays.

Should I check 'Include end day'?

Check this box if you want both the start day and the end day to count as full days. For example, Monday to Wednesday is 2 days difference, but 3 total days if both endpoint days are included.
