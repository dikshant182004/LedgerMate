Why Dog Size Significantly Changes Long-Term Costs

A 75 lb Labrador consumes roughly three times the calorie volume of a 15 lb Terrier. Medication dosages (flea, tick, heartworm, antibiotics, anesthesia) are also dosed strictly by body weight, making large dogs approximately 40% to 60% more costly to care for over their lifetime.
