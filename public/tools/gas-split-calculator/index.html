Road Trip Fuel Splitting Etiquette

Road trips are fantastic memories, but splitting fuel costs can get complicated if one person supplies the vehicle while everyone else rides along. Here are three standard ways to handle car expenses fairly:

1. The Passenger-Covered Gas Rule

When the driver uses their personal car, standard road etiquette suggests the passengers cover 100% of fuel and tolls. The car owner takes on the uncompensated cost of vehicle depreciation, mileage wear, tire tread, and cleaning.

2. The True Even Split (Car Rental)

If you rented a car via Hertz, Turo, or Enterprise, everyone (including the primary driver) splits the rental fee, fuel, insurance, and tolls equally down to the penny.
