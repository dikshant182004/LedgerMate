Home Loan Prepayment Math & Step-Up EMIs

1. The Early Prepayment Multiplier Effect

Because mortgage interest is calculated directly on the remaining loan principal, a part-prepayment made during the initial years delivers an outsized return compared to the same payment made toward the end of the loan:

The Timing Rule:
A ₹2,00,000 / $5,000 prepayment at Year 2 saves up to 12x more interest than the identical payment made at Year 16 because it prevents compounding interest across the entire intervening decade.

2. The Step-Up EMI Strategy

By increasing your monthly payment by just 5% each year (aligned with regular salary increments), you automatically direct the extra cash toward principal paydown without tightening your lifestyle:

Impact on a 20-Year Home Loan:
A standard 20-year loan at 8.5% interest is retired in just 11.4 years, saving over 40% of the total cumulative interest charges.

3. Reducing Balance vs Flat Rate Interest

Never accept a loan quoted with a flat interest rate. Under a flat rate loan, you continue paying interest on the entire original principal even when you have paid off 90% of the debt. Always ensure your loan contract specifies monthly reducing balance amortization.
