AI Calculation Agent Guide & Best Practices

Learn how our deterministic calculation pipeline blends natural language reasoning with guaranteed arithmetic execution and live Google Search Grounding.

1. Overview of the Architecture

Traditional Large Language Models (LLMs) often suffer from arithmetic hallucinations when computing compound interest, rent proration, or metrology conversions. LedgerMate uses a NeuroSymbolic pipeline:

  • Gemini Reasoning Engine: Extracts entities, formulas, and constraints from plain English/multilingual queries.
  • Google Search Grounding: Pulls real-time tax rates, currency rates, or metrological constants dynamically.
  • Deterministic V8 Math Engine: Executes exact recursive-descent math parser without risk of LLM arithmetic slips.

2. Getting Started in 3 Steps

Step 1: Sign in with Google
Tie your calculation records to your Google account for continuous history tracking.

Get a 100% free API key from Google AI Studio. Paste it in Key Settings.
Step 3: Ask in Any Language
Ask in English, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, etc. The agent interprets the query and outputs complete proofs in your selected language!

3. Example Queries You Can Try

# Unequal Roommate Rent Split "Split $3,800 rent between 3 people where Room A is master (+15%), Room B is standard, Room C is small (-10%)" # Metrology & Tax Computation "Convert 150 Gaj to Square Feet and calculate 18% GST at $2,400/sqft" # Loan Amortization "Calculate monthly EMI on a $450,000 mortgage at 6.5% interest for 30 years"

4. Interactive Sliders & Sensitivity Analysis

After every query, the agent generates interactive variable sliders. Adjusting any slider dynamically recalculates the headline result and the 5-point sensitivity matrix (-20% to +20%) in real time!
