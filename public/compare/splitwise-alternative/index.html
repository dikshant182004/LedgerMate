What Happened to Splitwise in 2025–2026?

For over a decade, Splitwise was the gold standard for splitting apartment groceries, dinner tabs, and vacation Airbnbs. However, in recent updates, Splitwise monetized its core experience aggressively:

  • The 3-Expense Daily Lockout: If you are on a weekend trip or running errands with roommates, logging breakfast, coffee, and lunch consumes your entire daily quota. Adding dinner prompts a paywall screen demanding a paid subscription.
  • The 10-Second Wait Screen: Free users report having to stare at artificial delay timers and banner ads before an expense can be saved.
  • Forced Friction for Friends: To split a single $15 pizza, everyone has to download a 100MB native app, verify an email account, and link their contacts.

The LedgerMate Philosophy: Splitting expenses is basic math. You should never be blocked from logging what you paid because of an arbitrary daily quota.
