FAQ

Common questions

Is LedgerMate really free?

Yes. There's no paid tier, no ads, and no daily limit on expenses. Quick anonymous groups auto-expire on a timer instead of being stored forever — that's what keeps the app sustainable to run for free.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Create a group and start splitting immediately. Signing in with Google is optional, and only needed for longer retention or to see all your groups in one place.

What happens to my expense data over time?

You choose the retention when you create a group — from 10 minutes up to a year, or permanent if you're signed in. Anonymous groups clean themselves up automatically once their window passes.

How is LedgerMate different from Splitwise?

Splitwise's free tier caps you at a few expenses a day, shows ads, and paywalls things like currency conversion behind Pro. LedgerMate has no daily limit, no ads, and syncs live across every device in the group.

Does everyone in my group need to download an app?

No. Share the link and anyone joins from a browser on any device — no app store, no install. It can optionally be added to a phone's home screen for an app-like feel.

Can I use LedgerMate on iPhone and Android?

Yes — it works in any modern mobile or desktop browser, and installs to your home screen on both iPhone and Android without an app store.

Can I keep a group's expenses permanently?

Yes — sign in with Google and choose "keep until I delete it" for up to two permanent groups per account.
